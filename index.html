My University Life — A Journey of Becoming

by Jupitara Baruah, 17-year-old BCA at Kaziranga University

18th August 2026 — The Day I Will Never Forget

I stepped into an unknown world. Everything was unfamiliar - the place, the faces, the air. On that day, even saying a simple 'hi' felt like the hardest thing in the world.

I was the girl who didn't have the courage to talk to the girl sitting right next to me.

But today... Those same strangers have become my own people. The place that felt so distant now feels like home. The girl who was once too shy to speak, can now stand on the university stage and speak confidently in front of hundreds.

This is more than just my university life. This is the story of me, finding myself. And I will save every chapter of these 4 years right here on this website.
